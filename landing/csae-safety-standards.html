SAFETY STANDARDS

Child Safety and CSAE Standards

Effective date: June 3, 2026

Alertly has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, trafficking facilitation, and any content or behavior that sexualizes minors.

1. Scope and Prohibited Content

The following are strictly prohibited on Alertly:

  • Any CSAM content, links, or attempts to distribute such material.
  • Any sexual content involving minors, real or fictional, including suggestive exploitation.
  • Grooming, coercion, sextortion, trafficking-related content, or attempts to contact minors for sexual purposes.
  • Any attempt to solicit, normalize, or promote child exploitation activity.

2. Detection and Moderation

  • Community reports are reviewed by moderation workflows and risk-based triage.
  • Flagged content may be removed immediately while review is pending.
  • Accounts engaged in CSAE violations are permanently banned.
  • We preserve relevant evidence where required by law and platform obligations.

3. Reporting Channels

Users can report suspected CSAE behavior by:

  • Using in-app reporting controls where available.
  • Emailing our safety contact at info@deningsy.com with subject: Urgent CSAE Report.

We prioritize CSAE reports for expedited review and take immediate restriction actions where needed.

4. Law Enforcement Cooperation

Alertly cooperates with valid law enforcement requests and reports actionable CSAE-related threats where legally required. We may share relevant account and activity information in compliance with applicable law.

5. Child Safety Point of Contact

  • Safety contact email: info@deningsy.com
  • Company: Alertly Community Systems

6. Policy Updates

We may update this safety standards page as regulations, platform requirements, or internal safeguards evolve. Material updates will be reflected by the effective date above.
